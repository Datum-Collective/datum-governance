This is not a legal company incorporation document

This is not an equity agreement

This is a coordination and alignment contract between 4 people.

This document governs participation in Datum Collective during Epoch 1.
It applies only to the period and members defined below and does not create obligations beyond this scope.


PURPOSE:

The purpose of Datum Collective during Epoch 1 is to collaboratively design, build, and ship technical projects for learning, experimentation, and proof-of-work.
Epoch 1 is exploratory in nature. It does not guarantee commercial output, profit, or continuation into future epochs.


MEMBERS:

The following individuals are members of Datum Collective during Epoch 1:

- Dan Thomas George — Cofounder
- Shivamsh S R — Cofounder
- Syed Abdul Hameed — Founding Engineer
- Agrajeet Sharma — Founding Engineer

Membership begins upon agreement to this document.

A member may leave Epoch 1 at any time by notifying the group.

A member may be removed from Epoch 1 by unanimous agreement of the remaining members.

Upon leaving or removal, a member retains no decision-making authority
within Datum Collective for Epoch 1.


DECISION MAKING:

Decisions affecting the collective as a whole require agreement by a majority of Epoch 1 members.

The following decisions require unanimous agreement:
- Admission of new members
- Removal of a member
- Commercial use of collective work
- Amendments to this agreement


WORK EXPECTATIONS:

Members are expected to contribute meaningfully to at least one project or initiative during Epoch 1.

Contribution may include design, development, research, documentation, or other agreed technical work.

Consistent non-participation may be grounds for removal, subject to the decision-making rules above.

INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY:

All work produced under Datum Collective during Epoch 1 is considered collectively owned by the Epoch 1 members.

No member may commercially use, license, or sell collective work without unanimous agreement of the members.


EQUITY AND COMPENSATION:

This agreement does not grant equity, ownership shares, or financial compensation to any member.

Participation in Epoch 1 does not imply entitlement to future equity,roles, or compensation unless agreed upon in a separate written agreement.


EPOCH DURATION:

Epoch 1 begins on the date this agreement is accepted by all members and ends upon mutual agreement or upon transition to a formally defined next epoch.

Upon termination of Epoch 1, no further obligations exist between members under this agreement.


AMENDMENTS:

Any changes to this agreement must be made in writing and approved according to the decision-making rules defined above.

All amendments must be committed to the shared repository.


ACKNOWLEDGEMENT:

By participating in Datum Collective during Epoch 1, each member acknowledges that they have read, understood, and agreed to this document.
