# Datum Collective — Epoch 1

This repository contains the coordination and alignment agreement that governed participation in Datum Collective during Epoch 1.

## Epoch 1 Status

**Epoch 1 has concluded.**

Datum Collective has transitioned to **Epoch 2**. The Epoch 1 agreement is retained in this repository as a historical record and is no longer the governing agreement for the collective.

The Epoch 1 agreement applies only to the members and period defined within it. Its historical membership and provisions have not been rewritten to reflect the later structure of Datum.

Current membership, roles, responsibilities, decision making, intellectual property, and other operating terms are governed by the Epoch 2 agreement.

## Archive Status

- `epoch-1-contract.md` is retained as the Epoch 1 agreement.
- Git history provides the record of amendments and changes made during Epoch 1.
- Epoch 2 documentation is located under `epoch-2/`.
- The Epoch 1 agreement should not be amended except where necessary to correct an archival or factual error.
