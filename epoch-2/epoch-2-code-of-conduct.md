# Datum Collective — Code of Conduct

**Epoch II**

1. **Be competent.**
   
   Know what you're doing. If you don't, learn it.

2. **Be honest.**
   
   Don't fake results, understanding, progress, or contribution.

3. **Challenge the work.**
   
   Disagree when you disagree. Bring evidence. Don't make technical disagreements personal.

4. **Do your part.**
   
   If you commit to something, follow through. If you can't, communicate it.

5. **Give credit.**
   
   Don't take ownership of someone else's work.

6. **Help each other.**
   
   Share knowledge, review work, and make use of the people around you.

7. **Don't be an asshole.**
   
   Technical ability, seniority, or position isn't an excuse to treat people badly.

8. **Keep it serious.**
   
   Datum is for doing the work. Don't let the organisation become more important than what it actually produces.
