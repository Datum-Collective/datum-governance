# Datum Collective — Epoch 2

Epoch 2 is the current operating framework of Datum Collective, effective 1 September 2026.

Epoch 2 marks the transition from Datum's initial exploratory phase into a structured engineering collective with defined roles, project ownership, technical responsibility, and governance.

The purpose of this directory is to contain the rules and agreements governing participation in Epoch 2.

## Documents

- `epoch-2-agreement.md` — governing agreement for Epoch 2

## Principles

Datum remains an engineering collective. Its work is centred on designing, building, testing, documenting, and improving technical systems.

The structure introduced in Epoch 2 exists to establish clear responsibility without creating unnecessary bureaucracy.

Technical authority should remain close to the work. Collective governance should determine matters that affect Datum as a whole.
